autointent.advisor.detect_hardware
==================================

.. py:function:: autointent.advisor.detect_hardware(*, vram_budget_gb = None, mps_budget_ratio = MPS_DEFAULT_BUDGET_RATIO)

   Detect the local hardware, with optional manual overrides.

   :param vram_budget_gb: when set, overrides the detected VRAM (use for
                          shared-GPU machines where part of the device is taken).
   :param mps_budget_ratio: fraction of total RAM treated as the MPS
                            "VRAM" budget on Apple Silicon.

   :returns: HardwareProfile reflecting current machine state.
